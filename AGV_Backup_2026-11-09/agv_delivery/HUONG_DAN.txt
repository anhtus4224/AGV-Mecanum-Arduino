BAN SUA AGV MECANUM - MAC DINH 190 PWM

1. Giai nen ZIP, mo agv_mecanum_chuan/agv_mecanum_chuan.ino trong Arduino IDE.
   Chon board Arduino Mega or Mega 2560, processor ATmega2560 (neu co muc nay),
   chon dung cong USB. Bam Verify, sau do Upload. Khong can thu vien Servo.
   Giu lai ban goc de quay lai neu can.

2. Thay doi:
   - Toc do thang toi da 190, tang 2 PWM moi chu ky dieu khien (danh nghia 10ms).
   - Cho on dinh 120ms luc bat dau / sau cua, sau do len 190 trong khoang 350ms.
     Chu ky thuc te co the dai hon do sieu am / serial.
   - Ba mat giua: lech nhe khong reset cruise; giam banh trong toi da 20 PWM.
   - Cham mat ria: vao cua ngay. Chi mat 2/4 bat cung phia lien tuc 100ms:
     cung vao cua, tranh giu toc do cao khi lech keo dai.
   - Thoat cua khi a<=1, khong mat ria, lien tuc 100ms; khong tang lai dot ngot.
   - Giu bang cua goc INNER={120,0,0,0,0}, OUTER={120,190,235,255,255}.
     LINE_BASE_SPEED=120 khong co nghia banh ngoai cua bi gioi han o 120:
     banh ngoai van co the len 255, banh trong ve 0. Chuyen tiep qua slew 40 PWM.
   - Tat xung phanh mac dinh. Khong dao chieu banh trong nhanh do line.
   - Mat line: lap tuc ha muc tieu hai ben xuong toi da 120, qua slew;
     tim lai bang ngoai 160, trong 0 neu huong cu ro. Het gio thi dung.
     Bat lai line phai qua giai doan on dinh truoc khi tang toc.
   - Khong co bo dem cu chay 1 giay la giam toc.
   - Hai mat ria cung bat: chay cham 120 thay vi coi trung binh err=0 la thang.
   - Giu nguyen chan, chieu cam bien, servo mem, toc do WiFi, thong so ne can.
     Chi bo sung reset trang thai bam line sau ne can / doi che do.
   - Sua them loi S HOA bi bo qua trong AUTO o ban goc: app gui S nay dung xe
     va thoat ve MANUAL. Giu nguyen y nghia s thuong cua giao thuc goc.

3. Thu xe:
   - Thu ke banh truoc de xac nhan chieu va lenh S dung duoc tu AUTO.
   - Thu san trong, nguoi dieu khien san sang dung. Chay thang, cua trai,
     cua phai, mat line va ne can. Khong coi test mo phong la test vat ly.
   - Mo Serial Monitor 115200 baud. HB co:
       cruise=190 zone=S a=0.00 L/R=190/190
     S=thang, C=cua, L=mat line, X=hai ria cung bat.
     a va L/R la gia tri tai luc HB (1 giay/lan); DATA van theo thu tu A0..A4.
     HB khong ghi duoc moi lan chuyen trang thai ngan.
   - Neu cua van vang, chua tang len 200. Gui log HB + DATA va video cua
     de dieu chinh SIDE_CURVE_MS / trim / muc toc do vao cua theo xe thuc.
   - Chi sau khi 190 on dinh moi doi:
       const int LINE_FAST_SPEED = 200;
     Khong doi WIFI_MAX_SPEED de tang toc do line.

4. Kiem tra da lam:
   - g++ -std=gnu++17 -Wall -Wextra -fsyntax-only voi stub Arduino.h: PASS.
   - Mo phong truc tiep sketch: 12 nhom tinh huong, toan bo 32 mau cam bien,
     10000 chu ky dau vao hon hop; assert huong PWM, tang toc, vao/thoat cua,
     mat/bat lai line, timeout, reset che do va lenh S AUTO: PASS.
   - AddressSanitizer + UndefinedBehaviorSanitizer: PASS (LeakSanitizer khong
     duoc ho tro trong sandbox, khong bat kiem tra ro ri bo nho).
   - Day la bien dich/kiem tra tren may chu voi stub, CHUA bien dich bang
     Arduino AVR toolchain, CHUA nap va CHUA chay thu tren xe.
   - Khong bao dam khong vang o PWM 190/200 truoc khi do/thu xe thuc.
