THAY DOI SO VOI BAN 190 TRUOC:
- BO SIDE_CURVE_MS: mat 2/4 bat line lau KHONG tu chuyen sang cua.
- BO dieu kien a<=1 de thoat cua. Chi mat 2/4 bat (a=2) van thoat cua duoc.
- BO cho CENTER_HOLD_MS va tang cruise tung buoc.
- Khi !L2 && !R2 va co line trong cum giua: cruiseSpeed dat truc tiep 190.
  Chu y: neu vua cua/mat line, cho hai ria sach va cum giua co line 60ms
  roi thoat trang thai cua, sau do dat truc tiep 190.
- Mat giua: muc tieu L/R=190/190.
  Mat giua + mat ben: 180/190 (hoac dao ben).
  Chi mat ben: 170/190 (hoac dao ben), khong khoa banh trong ve 0.
  Do la PWM muc tieu, khong phai toc do vat ly (m/s).
- Van giu slew 40 PWM/chu ky de motor khong nhay tu 0 len 190 tuc thi.
  Neu chu ky deu 10ms, tu 0 len muc tieu mat khoang 50ms.
- Cham mot mat ria: vao cua ngay, dung bang cua goc, nhung banh ngoai
  duoc gioi han toi da 190 (ban cu cho phep len 255). Banh trong co the
  ve 0 de be lai, khong dao chieu. Xung phanh van TAT.
- Sau khi roi ria: trong 60ms cho thoat cua, dung lai mem 120/100..120.
  Hai ria cung bat: chay cham 120, khong coi la thang de phong nhanh.
- Mat line: giam muc tieu hai ben toi da 120, tim lai o muc toi da 160,
  het thoi gian thi dung; khong giu lenh 190 trong 150ms mat line.
- Khong co giam toc theo thoi gian doan thang (1 giay/10 giay).
- Giu nguyen chan, REVERSE_SENSOR_ORDER, LINE_DETECTED, servo mem,
  giao thuc WiFi, thong so ne can va ban sua lenh S HOA dung AUTO.

DOC LOG DE PHAN BIET LENH 190 VA CHUYEN DONG THUC:
HB co fw=AGV_190_V2, cruise, zone, a, bits, target va L/R.
Chi xem gia tri bam line khi mode=AUTO.
bits thu tu logic TRAI -> PHAI: L2,L1,M,R1,R2 (da dao thu tu chan).
DATA giu thu tu chan A0..A4 nhu ban goc, nen KHONG dong nhat thu tu bits.
Vi du sau khi on dinh:
- bits=00100 zone=S cruise=190 target=190/190 L/R=190/190: giua line.
- bits=01000 zone=S cruise=190 target=170/190 L/R=170/190: chi mat trai giua.
- bits=00010 zone=S cruise=190 target=190/170 L/R=190/170: chi mat phai giua.
- zone=C: mat ria dang bat hoac dang cho thoat cua 60ms.
- zone=X: hai mat ria cung bat, chay cham.
- zone=L: mat line / da dung vi mat line.
HB moi 1 giay la anh chup thoi diem, khong ghi lai moi chuyen trang thai.
